Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

What We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your Google display name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile picture URL

We do not store your Google password or access any other Google data.

How We Use Your Data

  • Display your name and avatar in the forum, shoutbox, and profile pages
  • Identify your account for posting threads, replies, and chat messages
  • Send you in-app notifications when someone replies to your threads

Chat Assistant

Questions asked to the RO Game Database Assistant are logged anonymously to improve response accuracy. No personally identifiable information is attached to these logs.

Cookies

We use a single signed session cookie (forum_session) to keep you logged in. It expires after 7 days. We also store your dark mode preference in localStorage.

Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth — for authentication only
  • Twitch & YouTube APIs — to display streamer information (no user data is sent)

Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Forum posts and shoutbox messages are retained indefinitely. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy concerns or data deletion requests, reach out through the community forum or the chat assistant.
